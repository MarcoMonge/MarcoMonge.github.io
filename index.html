04 / About

Academic background

Download academic CV (PDF) ↓

My work sits between theoretical economics and empirical applications, with particular interests in international economics, production linkages, economic theory, and monetary economics.

I am a lecturer at the University of Costa Rica and have worked as a research assistant at the London School of Economics and Political Science. Earlier research experience includes projects at Princeton University, Yale University, and the University of Costa Rica's Institute for Research in Economic Sciences.

My academic training includes economics studies at the University of Costa Rica and graduate economics coursework at Universidad Torcuato Di Tella.
